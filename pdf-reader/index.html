ListenFlyiPhone app · built and operated by AyesTech

Your documents, read aloud – with AI voices that run on the phone.

PDFs, e-books, Word files, web articles and scanned pages become natural audio. Hundreds of AI voices in 35 languages run entirely on the device, offline, with no account and no upload of the user's library. ListenFly is our reference for what on-device AI can do on consumer hardware.

  • On-device neural text-to-speech in 35 languages
  • Word-by-word highlighting, screen-off listening
  • Camera scanning with text recognition
  • Privacy by design: no account, documents never leave the phone
