Questions

Which files can it read?

PDF, EPUB, DOCX and plain text, scanned PDFs, camera scans, web articles and pasted text.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The free device voice and the Standard AI voices work offline once a language is downloaded. Only Premium HD cloud voices and cloud text recognition need a connection.

Which languages are supported?

AI voices cover 35 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The app itself is available in more than 50 languages.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ListenFly. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Can I listen to any book?

ListenFly reads documents you own or are entitled to use. It hosts no content of its own.
